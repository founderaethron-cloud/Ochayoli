Manifesto / 001

Before there was a stage,
there was street.

Corners, bus stops, rooftops and riverbanks. Ochayoli exists to amplify voices that were already loud — the ones raised in Malayalam, rooted in place and built without permission.

Different voices. One restless spirit. Music that refuses to stay in its lane.

04 / The collective

O'Flow

The sound of what's next

O'Flow is a Malayalam hip-hop collective brought together by Ochayoli. Five artists, five directions: production, performance, freestyle, folk and the hard edge of techno.

Different roots, from Periyar to the Alappad coast to the Kochi streets, sharing one idea: Kerala's next sound is being written right now, in its own language.
